Housewarming Gifts for Renters (Useful, Under $30)

Which Gift Fits Your Friend?

Not sure which of the seven to pick? Match the gift to your friend:

  • They love to cook or host. Go with the wooden serving board or the cotton kitchen towels.
  • They're busy and still unpacking. A key and mail tray or a self-watering plant pot is useful right away and needs no setup.
  • They're a first-time renter. Damage-free adhesive hooks and a mini tool kit solve problems they will run into in the first month.
  • You don't know them well. Choose the kitchen towels or a small welcome mat: neutral, low risk, and easy to wrap.

How to Choose: Small, Useful, Easy to Use Right Away

Pick something small enough for a new apartment, useful enough to be used every week, and simple enough to use without tools or setup. Skip big decor, strong scents, and anything that needs to be mounted. Prices change often, so check the current price on Amazon before you buy.

1. Cotton Kitchen Towels

A set of cotton kitchen towels is the kind of gift people use daily and rarely buy for themselves. They brighten a new kitchen, and they're easy to wrap.

2. A Self-Watering Plant Pot

A self-watering plant pot is a good starter for someone who has a lot to do. It fits on a shelf or windowsill, and it's forgiving if the new owner forgets to water.

3. A Key and Mail Tray

A key and mail tray sits on a counter or small table and keeps keys, sunglasses, and mail in one spot. It's a small way to make a new entryway feel organized from day one.

4. A Welcome Mat

A small doormat makes a new front door feel like home. Pick a low-profile, neutral one that fits an apartment entry.

5. A Wooden Serving Board

A wooden serving board works as a cutting board, a cheese board, or a small decoration on a counter. It's a gift that looks nice and gets used.

6. Damage-Free Adhesive Hooks

Adhesive hooks let a renter hang keys, towels, or light decor without drilling. Check the weight rating and the wall surface, and test one spot first, since results vary on paint and wallpaper.

7. A Mini Tool Kit

A mini tool kit is a gift new renters appreciate when they need to assemble furniture or tighten a loose screw. Look for a compact set with a screwdriver, a small hammer, and a tape measure.

Before You Buy

  • Check their style. If you can, glance at their colors and kitchen. A neutral color is almost always a safe choice.
  • Think about pets. If your friend has a cat or dog, choose a pet-safe plant, since some common houseplants are harmful to pets.
  • Look at the size. A new apartment may have very little counter and shelf space, so pick items that fit in a drawer or on a shelf.
  • Add a gift receipt if you can. Many items offer a gift receipt or gift message at checkout, so your friend can swap it if it doesn't fit.
  • Order early. Shipping can take longer around the holidays, so order a week or two before you need it.

What to Avoid

Skip large decor, heavily scented candles or sprays, and anything that needs to be hung with nails. A new place is full of boxes, so the best gifts fit in a drawer or on a shelf.

Budget Tips

If you're only buying one gift, kitchen towels or a plant pot are the safest choices, since both are small, cheap, and used right away. For a bigger gift, pair two small items, such as a serving board and a set of towels. Leave the tool kit for friends who are still building furniture.
